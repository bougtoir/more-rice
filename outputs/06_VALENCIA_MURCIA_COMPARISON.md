# 06 — Valencia vs Orihuela/Murcia: Paired Comparison

Work Package D1. The two most similar conquest-and-repartition cases are compared on the variables that
could explain differential documentation. **Guardrail honoured: identical ecology/documentary conventions
are not presumed.**

## Comparison table

| Variable | Valencia (conquered 1238) | Orihuela/Murcia (repartimiento 1266–1314) |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-conquest rice | Attested in literature (al-Andalus huerta tradition; secondary attestation of repartiment arrozales — **folio unverified, A1**) | Murcia huerta comparable; rice attested in geographers of al-Andalus (secondary literature) |
| Repartition register | Reg. 5–7 (ACA): donations 1237–1252 + local notebooks | 87-folio codex, six partitions, 1266–1314; five Castilian + one Catalan |
| Rice entry in register | Claimed in secondary literature (unverified folio); NOT in Reg. 5 transcription (A1 negative) | No rice mention reported in scholarship on the codex; **codex text not accessible (A2 INACCESSIBLE)** |
| Irrigation regime | Intensive huerta; Islamic water institutions continued (verified, Furs/cartas puebla) | Vega Baja del Segura huerta; acequia system; water-rights scholarship exists (rua.ua.es study) |
| Population continuity | Mudéjar cultivators retained (documented) | Mudéjar presence also documented; different expulsion/resettlement dynamics |
| Crop vocabulary in register | jovatas, ortum, rahal, molendinis, furnis — land-use, not crop-specific | Comparable land-unit vocabulary; crop mentions limited in both genres |
| Document purpose | Recording royal donations (names, land, offices) | Recording allotments among settlers |

## What the contrast can and cannot show

- **Documentation asymmetry (verified):** Valencia's registers have *secondary-attested* rice claims;
  Orihuela's scholarship reports none. But both registers primarily record *land and grantees*, not
  crop inventories — a crop absent from a repartimento is not thereby absent from the land (guardrail 3).
- **Inference limit:** the pairing cannot establish that Orihuela lacked cultivation while Valencia had it.
  It establishes only that the two archives differ in what scholars have reported.
- **Honest status:** D0 on the Orihuela side; PARTIAL on the Valencia side (secondary attestation).
  Useful as a demonstration of *source-missingness structure*, unusable as a causal contrast.

## Contribution retained

The pair still does methodological work: it shows that "repartimento silence" is the *default state* of the
genre, and that the Valencia case's apparent positive evidence is itself unverified at folio level — so the
whole "continuity documented in the register" narrative is weaker than Phase 2 stated. The verified
continuity evidence is the **water-law layer**, not the crop layer.
