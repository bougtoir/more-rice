# 07 — Diffusion Within the Crown of Aragon

Work Package D2. Valencia, Catalonia, and Aragon shared a monarch (1238–1276 under James I) yet retained
autonomous legal regimes — a natural test of whether political unity sufficed for technology transfer.

## Evidence for actual administrative transfer

- **What did transfer:** the repartimento administrative genre (donations, jovata land units, register
  form); fueros/cartas puebla as legal instruments; royal chancery practice. Coded D7 for `admin` (E-ref
  in 04 edges).
- **What did not demonstrably transfer:** any rice cultivation item. No Catalan or Aragonese cultivation
  record, seed register, or Mudéjar-transfer order located in searched sources → D0.

## Why the same crown contained the crop

- **Legal autonomy:** Valencia kept its own Furs and water institutions; Catalan law (Usatges) and Aragonese
  fueros applied north of the border. Water-rights transplantation would have required explicit legal acts —
  none located.
- **Ecological boundary:** the Iberian System / Maestrazgo separates the Valencian huerta from interior
  Aragon (continental, winter-cold). The coastal Catalan plain (Delta de l'Ebre, Empordà) was theoretically
  plausible terrain — yet produced **no medieval rice record** in searched sources; the Ebre delta became a
  rice region in the 19th century. This is the strongest internal evidence that the binding constraint was
  *practical* (cultivators, water works, seed), not political: the same polity spanning a plausible zone
  generated no recorded attempt.
- **Personnel containment:** Mudéjar cultivators stayed where the water works were; no policy moved them
  and no searched source records them moving.

## Verdict for the case

Within one composite monarchy: institutional **templates** diffused; cultivation **practice** did not
demonstrably diffuse. Consistent with the labour-and-infrastructure interpretation (transfer requires moving
people + works, not documents) — stated as "not documented in searched sources," not as proof it never
happened.
