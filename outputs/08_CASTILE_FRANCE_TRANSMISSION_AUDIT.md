# 08 — Castile and France Transmission Audit

Work Package D3–D4. Routes: court contact, commerce, cultivator movement. Constraints from the Phase 2
climate sensitivity grid are imported, not recomputed.

## Castile

- **Dynastic channel:** Violant of Aragon m. Alfonso X (1244/46) — a queen, court, and household from a
  rice-growing realm in Castile for ~40 years. **No agronomic item attested moving with her** in searched
  sources (guardrail 2: marriage ≠ transfer).
- **Commerce:** edible rice circulated in Castile as an imported commodity (D1) — attested in cuisine and
  materia medica literature.
- **Cultivation evidence:** no Castilian cultivation record ≤1300 located → D0. Castilian repartimientos
  (Sevilla, Murcia) are land registers; no rice entries reported in scholarship searched.
- **Ecological constraint:** interior Castile (Meseta, continental winters) is climatically marginal
  per the sensitivity grid; Andalusian valleys (Guadalquivir) were theoretically feasible — and had
  Islamic-period antecedents — but no post-conquest cultivation record located. Not documented, not
  demonstrated absent.

## France

- **Dynastic channel:** Isabella of Aragon m. Philip III (1262); Treaty of Corbeil (1258) fixed the
  Pyrenean frontier. No agronomic transfer attested.
- **Languedoc/Roussillon:** inside the Mediterranean climate band (feasible at irrigation margins per the
  sensitivity grid); Roussillon was itself within the Crown of Aragon's orbit after 1258 — yet no
  cultivation record ≤1300 located → D0.
- **Northern France:** parametric-uncertain climate (11% of grid cells marginal per Phase 2); rice
  circulated only as commodity (D1, cookbooks). D0 for all cultivation stages.
- **England and Germany (distant comparisons):** used only as limiting cases. England/Germany infeasible
  across the entire parameter grid — their non-adoption is fully explained by climate and therefore can
  contribute no discriminating evidence to the mechanism analysis. Included to bound the comparison set,
  not as matched cases (guardrail: no forced matched inference).

## Pattern

At every border tested, diffusion documentation stops between **D1 (commodity)** and **D3 (actionable
knowledge)**. Nothing in searched sources documents a seed shipment, cultivator relocation, or irrigation
charter *exporting* Valencian rice practice before 1300. The chain of custody for the technology is
archaeologically and documentary invisible in this period — which is exactly what a *personnel-embedded,
non-textual* technology predicts.
