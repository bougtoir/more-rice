# 09 — Comparative Crops and Institutions (Negative Controls)

Work Package E. Three co-circulating items with independently verified 1000–1300 chronology, chosen to
isolate which constraint bound rice: an *institution* (irrigation governance), a *tree crop* (citrus), a
*processing crop* (sugarcane). A cereal (spelt/wheat) anchors the within-ecology contrast.

## Comparison

| Item | Diffusion 1000–1300 | Constraint binding vs rice |
|---|---|---|
| **Irrigation rules** (acequia rotation, water tribunals) | Documented continuity in Valencia (verified A/B); institutional transplantation attested between huertas (Cordova→Valencia lineage in literature) | Institutions are *portable*: they travel as legal text/custom without needing climate or personnel. Transmitted even where the crop did not. |
| **Citrus (sour orange)** | Diffused westward through al-Andalus; post-conquest continuity in Valencia huerta; presence in medieval Iberian records | Perennial, smallholder-compatible, no special water governance; cold-limited at similar latitude to rice — yet diffusion stopped at the *same* Pyrenean boundary. Supports ecological, not political, containment. |
| **Sugarcane** | Cultivated in Granada/Málaga Islamic period; continued post-conquest; never left the coast | Requires processing mills + hot coast; far more constrained than rice — its confinement confirms *ecology+processing* constraints as the normal binding mechanism for Iberian specialty crops. |
| **Heavy plough / three-field / watermill** (Phase 1 controls) | Diffused *within* strong coercive regimes (N. France, England) | Latifundia-friendly technologies adopted *under* serfdom — refutes a general suppression mechanism; bound only for rice-like crops needing different ecology. |

## Within-region contrast (the cleanest test)

Inside the Valencian huerta itself: water institutions (D7) and citrus (D6) demonstrably persisted under
Christian rule, while *rice documentation* sits at D0 (unverified secondary attestation). Same place, same
regime, same period — different diffusion documentation by item. The differentiating factor is the item's
attachment to *specific wetland personnel and works*, not political permission.

## Verdict

Controls eliminate the candidate mechanism "political/dynastic blockage" at the network level: items that
needed only documents or trees moved or persisted; the item that needed *cultivator communities +
continuous water works* did not demonstrably move. The binding constraint is infrastructural-personnel, not
institutional.
