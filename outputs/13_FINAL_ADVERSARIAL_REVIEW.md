## Reviewer A — Medieval historian (documentary criticism)

**FATAL (unresolved):** The flagship claim "the Llibre del Repartiment registers arrozales" cannot be
verified; worse, the Reg. 5 transcription is provably rice-free. Any manuscript asserting it without the
PARES folio check would publish an unverified — possibly false — primary claim. *Status: forces the paper
to drop that exhibit entirely or queue behind archive access.*

**MAJOR:** "Diffusion stopped at the practical layer" is an argument *from missing sources*. The reviewer's
own ontology (11) admits D0 is expected even if transfer occurred — so the layered-diffusion narrative
describes the archive, not the world. Acceptable only if the article is explicitly about *documented*
diffusion.

**MINOR:** Orihuela codex never examined (A2 INACCESSIBLE); calling the pair a "comparison" overstates it.

## Reviewer B — Agrarian historian / agronomist

**MAJOR:** The mechanism matrix (10) concedes M10 (economic unattractiveness) is undertested — the simplest
rival explanation (wheat systems adequate; rice only wins inside expensive huerta) is not discriminated from
personnel-containment. Both predict identical observations.

**MAJOR:** Climate grid still cannot discriminate at the *margins* (Ebre delta feasible, no adoption) —
which is where the interesting question lives. The audit answers "why not England" (climate) but not
"why not Roussillon/Ebre."

**MINOR:** Citrus/sugarcane are weak comparators (perennial, processing-heavy); the best comparator —
irrigated *vegetables/alfalfa* in the same huertas — was not included.

## Reviewer C — Historical political economist

**MAJOR:** "Labor margin vs technology margin" is a nice formulation but the 1388 statute is 88–140 years
after the window and English — using it as the *counterfactual mechanism* is chronologically and
geographically mismatched with Valencia. It illustrates a general logic; it is not evidence about Iberian
rice.

**MINOR:** Domar-extension claim (rulers bind labor, not output) is conceptually clean but empirically
tested only against a null (zero suppression documents).
