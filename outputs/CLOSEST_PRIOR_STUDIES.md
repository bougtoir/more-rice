## Novelty assessment

- **A. New primary-source evidence?** No new manuscripts discovered; audit adds the *forgery-exclusion* correction (Vella rescript) to the standard narrative — a methodological cleaning, not new evidence.
- **B. New comparative dataset?** **Partially yes** — the institutional-comparison matrix (region × regime × knowledge-level × cultivation outcome, with forgery-filtered Sicily) does not exist in this form.
- **C. New causal mechanism?** No — the posited suppression mechanism is contradicted; a *defensible* mechanism emerging from the audit is the inverse: coercion binds labor, high-yield crops are promoted when feasible.
- **D. New synthesis?** Yes — the K0–K4 knowledge ladder + A–D non-adoption classification + forgery audit + Domar mechanism test is a genuinely new framing of a scattered literature (Arab agronomy, archaeobotany, manorial accounts, Hanseatic trade).
- **E. Verdict:** novelty rests on synthesis (D) + structured comparative evidence (B). Sufficient for a *negative-result institutional study*, not for confirming the original hypothesis.
