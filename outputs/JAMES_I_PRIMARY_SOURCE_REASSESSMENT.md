## Corrections applied to the previous audit

1. **Previous claim downgraded.** Statements that the Furs/cartas puebla "protected rice cultivation" overreached: they protect **water rights and tenure (A/B)** generically. Rice promotion follows only when combined with the Llibre del Repartiment evidence that arrozales existed within that protected regime. The correct formulation: *"James I's settlement protected the irrigation institutions within which rice was grown, and the repartition registers rice fields as existing allotments (C)."* There is no document authorizing, promoting, or funding rice specifically (D/E/F = not established).

2. **New item added.** The **Llibre del Repartiment** is the single most important document for Task 1: it upgrades the evidence from "irrigation protection only" to "rice fields explicitly registered" — but as *existing* fields, i.e., continuity (C), not promotion (E/F).

3. **New negative case added.** The **Orihuela/Murcia** case (S28): post-conquest documentation is silent on rice where Arabic geographers implied earlier cultivation. Two readings: (a) rice cultivation actually declined/disappeared after 1243 (population displacement, irrigation change); (b) it persisted marginally below documentary notice. Either way it breaks any uniform "continuity under Christian rule" claim — continuity was real in Valencia city/huerta, uncertain-to-negative in the southern huertas.

4. **Interpretation corrected in CULTIVATION_EVIDENCE.csv and POLITICAL_INTENT_EVIDENCE.csv** (P01 revised): James I evidence = institutional continuity (A/B) + explicit registered cultivation (C via Llibre del Repartiment). Still evidence *against* suppression, but no longer describable as "promotion of rice."

## Evidentiary limitations

- The Furs and cartas puebla texts were consulted through published editions and secondary quotation (UAlicante 2002; HistAgrar 2021), not the manuscript registers.
- The Llibre del Repartiment rice entries are attested in secondary literature (DO Arroz de Valencia historiography; ACA catalogue); the audit did not independently collate folios — a flagged deficiency for any GO (see FINAL_MANUSCRIPT_GO_NO_GO).
- The Orihuela silence is survival- and registration-dependent: repartimentos record *distributed* products, and rice may have been consumed locally rather than tithed/distributed.
