# NOVELTY AND JOURNAL FIT

## What a publishable version could claim (honest framing)

A publishable paper *cannot* claim "medieval rulers suppressed rice." It can defensibly claim:

> "Rice entered medieval Western Europe's policy space nowhere: adoption failure is fully explained by the interaction of thermal limits, irrigation institutions, and the Arabic–Latin knowledge barrier. Where cultivation was technically feasible, rulers *promoted* or *protected* it (al-Andalus → Valencia continuity; Sforza promotion 1475). The peasant-dependence suppression mechanism fails both the documentary record and landlord-incentive logic."

That is a real, novel negative result with a new comparative apparatus (K-ladder, A–D non-adoption classes, institutional matrix, forgery-cleaned Sicily case).
