# RENAISSANCE ITALY — DISCUSSION DECISION (Task 8)

## Decision: INCLUDE as a short, qualified Discussion subsection

## Justification

The 1470s–1490s Milanese–Ferrarese introduction is the only documented *decision
event* in European rice history: a named ruler, a dated letter (27 Sep 1475),
physical seed transfer, subsequent measurable expansion (Ferrara market prices
by 1495). It shows what adoption actually required when it finally happened
north of the Mediterranean: ducal experimentation + reclamation context + seed
diplomacy + commercialization — and it happened *outside* the study period and
outside the serfdom question.

## What it contributes to the medieval analysis

1. **Mechanism validation, not projection**: the factors that delivered adoption
   in the Po Valley (knowledge via persons/seed gift, irrigation investment,
   revenue incentive) are precisely the factors the typology identifies as
   missing in medieval NW Europe. The later success corroborates the earlier
   failure-analysis without being used as evidence of medieval intent.

2. **The export-ban asymmetry**: Milan's one restriction was on seed *export* —
   monopoly protection of a promoted crop — documenting that rulers' rice
   interventions ran toward capture of surplus, not suppression of output.

## Explicit disclaimers carried into the Discussion

- No inference from 15th-c. Italy to 11th–13th-c. rulers' motives.
- No causal role claimed for Renaissance humanism (temporal coincidence only).
- One paragraph maximum in any manuscript; omitted entirely if reviewers object.
