# Modern Rice Global Expansion — Research Question & Hypotheses

## Objective
Under modern cultivars, cultivation systems, knowledge transmission, and logistics: where in the world's
non-traditional regions could rice expansion be rational on food-security, resource-efficiency, farm-income,
or environmental grounds — and where should it NOT be recommended.

Expanding rice is the research motivation, not the conclusion. Where rice loses to
wheat/barley/maize/existing crops, we say so.

## RQs (from spec)
- RQ1: where is rice biophysically feasible under modern technology (incl. former wheat regions)?
- RQ2: where feasible, where can rice beat incumbents on edible energy/revenue/resource/stability?
- RQ3: which system fits — upland, rainfed lowland, irrigated lowland, direct-seeded, AWD (defined
  strictly; systems overlap)?
- RQ4: how far do variety/sowing/water/labor/market improvements shift the feasible frontier?
- RQ5: where agronomically promising but unadopted, how much can barriers be explained by data?
- RQ6: where is rotation/addition (double-crop, fallow) more rational than conversion?

## Working hypotheses (testable, falsifiable)
- H1: A non-trivial band of currently non-rice temperate regions is *thermal-feasible* under modern
  early-maturing/cold-tolerant cultivars but remains rainfed- or irrigation-limited.
- H2: Where feasible, rice's edible-kcal/ha advantage is real but smaller than naive expectation (paddy→
  milled discount; comparable to best wheat in warm-temperate Europe, not 3–4×).
- H3: The binding constraints in feasible-but-unadopted regions are infrastructural/institutional
  (water works, milling, seed systems, subsidies) rather than biophysical — testable via the
  A–E barrier classification.
- H4: In high-latitude regions, rotation insertion (summer rice after winter cereal) is more plausible
  than full conversion.

## Prior-registered focal sets (spec §7)
- Screening set: global 10-arcmin WorldClim grid (all land).
- Non-traditional candidate regions: England, N. France, Germany, Nordic fringe, E. Europe — NOT presumed
  suitable.
- Positive controls: Japan, Camargue (France), Po Valley (Italy), Spain (Ebro/Andalusia), USA (California/
  Gulf), Bangladesh, Egypt.
- Negative controls: far-N. Scandinavia, arid-interior zones without water, highland tropics.
