## Discrimination result (RQ5)
- The historically-asked question ("why didn't N. Europe adopt rice?") resolves as **A** — the barrier
  literature only matters once class C/D is established.
- Genuine "feasible-but-unadopted" puzzle zones: Lower Danube/Balkans, steppe margins, Central Asia —
  where the binding constraint is capital works + water institutions + processing chain, **not climate and
  not suppression**.
- Medieval dynastic-network findings are explicitly NOT reused as variables (spec §8); only the
  *structural lesson* (technology lives in people+works, not texts) transfers as background.
