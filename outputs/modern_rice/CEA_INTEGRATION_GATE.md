# CEA Integration Gate
Decision: **CEA-SEPARATE** — exploratory CEA evidence does not alter field-rice conclusions and is excluded from mandatory gates; indoor feasibility does not reverse outdoor cold-region exclusions.
Rationale: no independently verified edible-grain yields at scale; energy floor ~$10/kg dry matter (Plant Physiology 2025) >> field rice ~$0.4/kg; grid dependency adds systemic fragility. Residual value: possible small emergency-reserve niche — requires an independent engineering study, not this manuscript.
