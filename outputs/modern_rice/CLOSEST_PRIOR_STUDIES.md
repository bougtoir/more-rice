# Closest Prior Studies (Phase 5)

1. van Oort & Zwart 2018, *Food Policy* — global rice agro-climatic suitability (basis of our screen).
2. van Oort et al. 2015, *Nat. Clim. Change* — cold-region rice; documents Hokkaido-type adaptation.
3. Shimono et al. — flowering-stage cold damage in northern Japan (cold-tolerance physiology).
4. Kuroda et al. — Hokkaido varietal history (ultra-early japonica).
5. Fodor et al. 2017 — global cropland suitability under climate change.
6. You et al. / MapSPAM 2017+ — spatial production allocation (validation baseline).
7. Siebert et al. 2015 — GMIA v5 irrigation extent.
8. Mekonnen & Hoekstra 2011 — blue/green water footprint per crop.
9. Bouman et al. / AWD literature — water-saving rice systems.
10. Gaupp et al. / Bren d'Amour — food-system tail-risk & diversification.
11. Meuwissen et al. — resilience assessment frameworks for farming systems.
12. Tendall et al. 2015 — food-system resilience definition.
13. Licker et al. 2010 — global crop gap analysis.
14. Portmann/GAEZ — agro-ecological zoning.
15. Kim et al. — CH4/AWD environmental accounting.

**Claimed narrow novelty:** cultivar-aware, water-constrained, economically bounded assessment that quantifies food-security TAIL-RISK reduction with a mandatory non-rice comparator — the simultaneous 4-way constraint at marginal-temperate scale; not the components individually.
