# Cultivation-System Water Gates
- W-A irrigated lowland: PASS for Lower Danube (Q99 1100 m3/s >> ~24 m3/s at 250kha), conditional elsewhere; infrastructure required.
- W-B rainfed upland: **FAIL for continental Europe** — daily bucket balance (TAW100mm, Hargreaves ET0) shows flowering-stage deficit >10d in 88-100% of years (LowerDanube 100%, Osijek 100%, Kherson 100%, Loire 100%, Beauce 100%); only Japan (Hokkaido 48%, Morioka 20%) and Denmark/Lund (64-52%) fall below. Rainfed upland CANNOT bypass the water gate in continental climates.
- W-C supplemental: conditional — supplemental demand ~every other year at flowering; quantified demand rows in DAILY_SOIL_WATER_BALANCE.csv; infrastructure still required.
