# Lower Danube Water Balance
- Danube mean discharge at Iron Gate ~5,500-6,500 m3/s; Romanian section; SDG 6.4.2 Romania 7.6% (low stress).
- Existing irrigation: Danube basin historically irrigated; GMIA regional AEI ~11%; refurbishable Soviet-era systems documented.
- Estimates (scenario class, no mass-balance model): flooded rice ~800-1200mm/crop consumptive; AWD documented 15-30% savings with 0-15% yield penalty; drought-year constraints real (2003/2015 low flows).
- Conclusion: water physically abundant at country scale, allocation/infrastructure is binding constraint → field-trial tier supported; basin-scale mass balance remains an engineering study, flagged not done.
