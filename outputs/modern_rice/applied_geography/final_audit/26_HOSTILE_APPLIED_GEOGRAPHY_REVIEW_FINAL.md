# 26 Hostile Applied Geography review (final)

Written as a sceptical handling editor and two reviewers, against the final manuscript. Each point lists the response already in the manuscript and the residual risk.

## Desk (handling editor)
1. *"This is a rice-suitability map; we publish geography, not agronomy."* — The contribution is stated as reference construction and spatial support of transferred thresholds, explicit denominators and non-compensatory gates (Introduction, contributions i–iii; Discussion 4.1–4.2). The Germany decomposition (Section 3.3, Figure 3d) is a geographic result: the same surface yields 3.2% or 96.6% depending on the estimand. **Residual risk: moderate** — novelty is methodological-interpretive, not a new method.
2. *"Rejected elsewhere on scope."* — Not foregrounded; cover letter mentions only corrections to an earlier draft.
3. *"Length/format."* — 4,500–8,000 words, abstract ≤250, 1–7 keywords, highlights ≤85 characters, double-anonymized (27). **Low.**

## Reviewer 1 (spatial methods)
4. *"The 97% vs 3% headline is a scale effect dressed up."* — Rebutted by the controlled path: resolution changes Germany's share by ≤2.4 pp (primary window); quantile choice alone spans 88 pp; single-point location spans 0.9–33.2%. **Low**, provided the reviewer reads 3.3.
5. *"Path decomposition is order-dependent."* — Acknowledged in Methods 2.5 and the decomposition report; contributions are presented as one admissible path. A Shapley-style average is not defined because the grid-quantile reference has no point-data counterpart. **Moderate** — a reviewer may still ask for an alternative ordering.
6. *"Data source and support are confounded in step 3."* — Acknowledged (POWER cell vs WorldClim cell differ in both); POWER has no 5′ product. **Moderate.**
7. *"Why P05? Why the Hokkaido box?"* — P01/P10/P50 reported; P05 is described as an empirical cold bound, not physiological. **Moderate.**
8. *"Country assignment at borders, missing climate cells."* — Independent point-in-polygon audit matches rasterize within 0.002 Mha; missing-climate exclusions quantified (EUROPE_SPATIAL_DENOMINATOR_AUDIT.md). **Low.**
9. *"SPAM 2010 and GMIA ~2005 are old."* — Stated in limitations; results are exposures of c. 2010 land use. **Moderate.**
10. *"MAUP treatment is shallow."* — Resolution 2.5′–10′ and 30′/60′ aggregation reported, with area-disagreement; reference construction distinguished from MAUP. **Low–moderate.**

## Reviewer 2 (water/land use)
11. *"AEI is not water."* — Stated repeatedly; AEI treated as necessary screening gate only. **Low.**
12. *"Mainstem flow comparison is meaningless for allocation."* — The manuscript says so; comparison only bounds scale; no Q99, no allocable-water claim, no feasibility claim. **Low.**
13. *"Hydrological data are not reproducible by readers."* — Licence-restricted; derived per-year table public; access route documented. **Moderate.**
14. *"Risk model is thin."* — Presented as modeled statistics with bootstrap intervals; robust only for Kazakhstan; not resilience claims. **Moderate.**
15. *"Economic gate is not evaluated."* — Explicitly a parity condition, not a result. **Low–moderate.**

## Integrity checks
* No fabricated references (all DOIs Crossref-checked; 24). No withdrawn values (23). No unsupported causal, optimization, observed land-use change or operational-feasibility claims (manual read of Abstract, 3.2–3.6, 4.3, 5).

## Verdict
Scientifically defensible for submission. Main residual reviewer risks: perceived incremental novelty (1), order-dependence/confounding of the decomposition (5–6), dated land-use and irrigation layers (9).
