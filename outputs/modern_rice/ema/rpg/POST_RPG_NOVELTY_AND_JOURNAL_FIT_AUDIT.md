# Post-RPG novelty and EM&A fit

## What RPG adds
- Converts the previously stated limitation 'no independent observation' into a tested statement for France: declared rice (13.7–19.6 kha) is never omitted at T* and at most 0.02% at 900/1100 GDD10.
- Shows that MapSPAM's French omission (12.1% at T*) arises from allocation outside the declared district (4.1% of MapSPAM French rice lies in RPG cells), supporting the manuscript's existing low-density-margin interpretation with independent evidence.
- Reinforces, rather than overturns, the central claim: independent presence data are also unable to rank references because they occupy only warm cells.

## What RPG does not add
- No discrimination between T* and 900 GDD10; no specificity; one delta only; no new author-collected field data.

## Effect on desk-rejection risk 1 (field-data preference)
Reduced from HIGH to MEDIUM–HIGH: the paper now includes an independent, field-declared parcel dataset, but it is administrative open data rather than author-collected monitoring. The novelty boundary (empirical reliability assessment, not a new method) is unchanged.
