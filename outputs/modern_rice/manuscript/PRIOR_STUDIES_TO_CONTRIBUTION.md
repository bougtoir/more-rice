# Prior Studies -> Contribution
Closest: van Oort & Zwart (2018) = suitability mapping; our increment = cultivar/management-aware feasibility + system-separated water gates + tail-risk + economics. Renard & Tilman (2019) = generic diversification stabilizes production; ours = crop-specific, comparator-tested application with feasibility gates. Gaupp (2020)/Mehrabi & Ramankutty (2019) = synchronized failure; ours = candidate-region feasibility joined to those failure statistics. Monaco (2016)/Guglielmo (2025) = Italian observed costs; ours = break-even transfer, not invented margins. Novelty is the integrated, bounded application — not a new principle.
