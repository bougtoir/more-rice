## Recommendation: Land Use Policy
Reason: AG confirmed scope fit and rejected on novelty. LUP values decision-relevant land-use evidence over methodological novelty, and the manuscript's strongest finding is policy-shaped: a thermal threshold is a weak screen for crop-transfer proposals, while irrigation infrastructure and allocable water decide where a rice system could function (16.0% of incumbent arable land passes both mapped gates; Germany's thermal pass share swings 3.2–96.6% with reference construction alone). Reframing needed (no new numbers): a policy question in the title/introduction, a "Implications for land-use screening" section replacing parts of 4.3, double-anonymized files, highlights ≤85 characters. Scientific results stay frozen.
